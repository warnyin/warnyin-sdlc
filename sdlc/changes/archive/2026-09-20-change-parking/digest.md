- **Shipped**: `journal.mjs park <id>` / `unpark <id>`, reason on stdin as `{"reason": "..."}` and no argument form at all. A parked change keeps its stage, leaves the listing, the ordering, next work and the freed-but-not-resumed flag, is counted and listable with `--all`, and refuses to be made active or shipped. Nothing may be stranded behind one, from either direction.
- **Specs merged**: `change-parking` (new, 3 requirements) + `change-relations` (1 ADDED, 2 MODIFIED) + `change-focus` (1 MODIFIED), no drift. The new spec was again born with an empty `## Purpose` — the header `new.md` §3 greps to ground the next delta — and again hand-written at ship. Second time running, so it is mechanical, not bad luck; `specs/verify-gates/spec.md` still carries the same placeholder.
- **Two defects that withdrew this feature once came back and were proved by running before being fixed**: `.state/journal` as a symlink carried the event, with the reason in it, OUT of the project; and `unpark` printed success and exited 0 while the change stayed parked, because the remover drops the first duplicate key and the reader resolves the last. A third: a `change.md` that could not be written exited 0 in silence, since the hook's fail-open catch also swallowed a command's failure.
- **The write path was rebuilt, not patched**: `pathIsContained` walks every existing segment to the target; `writeFileContained` re-checks immediately before the write and opens with `O_NOFOLLOW` so a symlink swapped in after the check is refused by the kernel; the journal append got the same two layers. Park and unpark each prove their own result, and every failure exits non-zero naming the change.
